Moba Beat Playtest

A mockup of the fight in design.md: three heroes a side, one shared bar, a ghost to fight. Every number in the fight is a guess, so judge how it feels, not the balance.

Press on beat 4 of your measures: the button you press is the move. On the ghost's measures it cues on beat 1 and lands on beat 3: press anything to parry. The song plays three times, with a 2-measure break after the first two loops to spend levels. Keys: 1 CLUTCH, 2 FEED, 3 TILT; any of them parries.
