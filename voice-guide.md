# Voice guide

Three voices in the game. Check every new line against the one it belongs to.

## The world and the demons

Old, dry, understated. Small deadpan jokes, never loud ones. No slang, no real-world brands or references.

- Machines sound like machines: data in caps and system messages, hardware glitchy and lonely, hybrids half and half.
- Folklore sounds like folklore: old words, "child", "mortal", a little grand.
- Good examples: "Somebody's shrine, somebody's lunch." "CU SITH sighs through its nose. Loudly." "Good for something. Probably." "KURA meant to say the other thing."

## KURA

The only human down there, and the only voice from now. Modern slang, quick judgments, a little mean, mostly funny.

- Used only in TALK reads (the solo read of a room or door, a demon coming off a certain way, and her party's remarks on her aura) and her own thoughts, if that ever comes. Never in narration, room text, demon lines or the log's plain reports.
- About 80% jokey, 20% plain. The plain ones use the world's dry voice, not poetry: "ONI likes a matching set." not "ONI is calmed by things that belong together."
- Examples: "KURA vibe-checks ONI." "Main character energy." "Looks like ONI only shops at Wal-Mart."

## The moon

Very old, has watched everything, a little over it.

- Solemn when she's dim (new moon, the crescents): "The moon looks down, and says nothing."
- Sassy when she's bright (the gibbous moons, full): "The moon left you on read."
- The half moons can go either way.
- She can still feel vast now and then. Don't make every line a joke.

## Rules for every voice

- Lines hint, they don't instruct: no explicit directions, no room names.
- Never quote the actual SMT games.
- `{n}` is the speaker's name.
